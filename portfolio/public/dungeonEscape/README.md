## How to Take Screenshots from Unreal Engine

1. Open your DungeonEscape project in Unreal Engine
2. Navigate to MainLevel or the level you want to capture
3. Press **G** to toggle Game View (hides editor UI)
4. Position the camera for a good shot showing:
   - The dungeon environment with candles and lighting
   - The character in the scene
   - Interesting gameplay areas (doors, collectibles, puzzles)
   - The atmospheric dark dungeon setting
5. Press **F9** to take a screenshot
6. Find screenshots in: `DungeonEscape/Saved/Screenshots/Windows/`
7. Copy the best 5 screenshots and rename them as listed above
8. Place them in this folder

The screenshots I saw show a beautiful dark dungeon atmosphere with candles, character, and medieval setting - capture similar scenes!
