## How to Take Screenshots from Unreal Engine

1. Open your ObstacleAssault project in Unreal Engine
2. Navigate to MainLevel
3. Press **G** to toggle Game View (hides editor UI)
4. Position the camera to show:
   - The obstacle course with moving platforms
   - The bright, colorful Asian-inspired environment
   - Different sections of the level (river, buildings, platforms)
   - The platforming challenges
5. Press **F9** to take a screenshot
6. Find screenshots in: `ObstacleAssault/Saved/Screenshots/Windows/`
7. Copy the best 3 screenshots and rename them as listed above
8. Place them in this folder

The screenshots I saw show a vibrant world with Asian architecture, river, and moving obstacles - capture similar dynamic scenes!
